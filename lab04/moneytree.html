Why I Love Money Trees

I chose a jpeg of a money tree because they are my second favorite type of plant.
Money trees symbolize wealth, prosperity, and good luck.
A money tree can live long enough to be passed down generations.
When they are young, the trunks are hand-braided. As they grow, the trunks fuse together.
